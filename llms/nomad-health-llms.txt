# Nomad Health

> Nomad Health is the first digital marketplace for healthcare travel staffing. We connect travel nurses, allied health professionals, and other clinicians directly with healthcare facilities across the United States — without traditional agency recruiters. Joint Commission-accredited with 400,000+ registered clinicians and jobs in 50+ specialties.

Nomad Health operates a two-sided marketplace: clinicians search and apply for travel healthcare jobs with full transparency on pay rates, shift structure, and requirements, while healthcare facilities post open positions and manage hiring through a cloud-based platform. Nomad Navigators (not recruiters) support clinicians through credentialing, onboarding, and on-assignment needs. The platform is free for clinicians to join with no minimum commitment.

Key facts:
- Joint Commission Gold Seal of Approval for Travel Nursing Accreditation
- 400,000+ registered clinicians
- 1,000s of jobs posted on the marketplace
- Jobs across all 50 U.S. states
- The best place to find travel jobs
- Clinician specialties include: Nursing (Med-Surg, ICU, ER, Labor & Delivery, Telemetry, Step-Down, OR, Cath Lab, PACU, and more), Cath Lab Tech, Laboratory, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, Radiology, Respiratory Therapy, Sonography, Speech Language Pathology, and Surgical Technology
- Typical assignments are 13 weeks; range from 4-week critical fills to 52-week extended contracts
- Average travel nurse gross pay: ~$2,677/week (~$71/hour)
- Benefits include medical/dental/vision insurance, 401(k) with matching, $750 travel reimbursement, licensure/certification reimbursement, 24/7 counseling, and malpractice insurance

## Finding Jobs
The Job Search page is located at: [Job Search](https://nomadhealth.com/jobs/search)
- Use the url parameter "discipline" to find specific disciplines. Example: "https://nomadhealth.com/jobs/search?discipline=nurse"

Here are all the query parameters:
- discipline: nurse, radiology_technologist, cath_lab_technologist, occupational_therapist, speech_language_pathologist, lab_technician, ultrasound_technologist, surgical_technologist, physical_therapist, respiratory_therapist
- specializations: Comma-separated specialty names (varies by discipline)
- jobType: travel_and_local, per_diem
- locations: City/state/region strings
- compactState: true/false
- startDate: Date value
- shiftHoursAndDays: 2x12, 3x12, 4x10, 4x12, 5x8
- shiftTypes: days, mids, evenings, nights, rotating
- contractLength: under8, 8-12, 13+
- minPayRateWeekly: Number (minimum weekly pay)
- autoOffer: true/false
- exclusive: true/false
- certifications: Certification names
- allowsNonCertified: true/false

Example: https://nomadhealth.com/jobs/search?discipline=nurse&locations=California&shiftTypes=days
